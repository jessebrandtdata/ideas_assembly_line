Bill Delay Tracker

Lag between a bill text version on GovInfo and its CRS summary on Congress.gov.

Lag is measured to the summary's last-update date, the only date the Congress.gov API exposes; a summary revised later reads slower than it was. The text date is the date printed on the version. "Waiting" means no summary at that stage appears in the data collected.
